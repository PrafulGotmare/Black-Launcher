# Privacy Policy – Black Launcher

Black Launcher does not collect, store, share or transmit any personal data.

- The app has no internet permission and cannot send data anywhere.
- It reads the list of installed apps on your device only to show them on screen.
- Your pinned and hidden apps are saved only in the app's private storage on your phone.
- The optional "Device admin" permission is used only to lock the screen when you double-tap. It cannot wipe, monitor or control anything else.
- No analytics, advertising or third-party libraries are included.
